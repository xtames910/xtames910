### 🚀 Projects

**📉 Stock Anomaly Detection on the Vietnamese Market** · `Python` `TensorFlow` `statsmodels`
Rebuilt FineNet (dilated CNN + BiGRU) to forecast weekly ROI and flag abnormal weeks on ACB Bank prices since 2017. At 1.0σ it caught 94% of anomalies versus 32% for the best of ARIMA, GRU and LSTM. → [finenet](https://github.com/xtames910/finenet)

**⚖️ Income Prediction and Fairness Audit** · `scikit-learn` `Fairlearn` `SHAP`
Leakage-safe Random Forest on 30,162 UCI Adult records, audited by sex and race. Mitigation cut the parity gap from 0.154 to 0.004 for 2.2 points of accuracy, and I report what it did not fix. → [Adult-Income](https://github.com/xtames910/Adult-Income)

**🛍️ Customer Segmentation on Walmart Data** · `Python` `K-Means` `PCA`
Led a team of 3 to turn 50,000 customers into 4 segments, each with a retention or remarketing move.
