### 🚀 Projects

**📉 Stock Anomaly Detection on the Vietnamese Market** · `Python` `TensorFlow/Keras` `statsmodels`
Rebuilt FineNet (dilated CNN + BiGRU) to forecast weekly ROI and flag abnormal weeks on ACB Bank prices since 2017, with a time-ordered 80/20 split. At 1.0σ it caught 94% of anomalies versus 32% for the best of ARIMA, GRU and stacked LSTM. → [finenet](https://github.com/xtames910/finenet)

**⚖️ Income Prediction and Algorithmic Fairness Audit** · `Python` `scikit-learn` `Fairlearn` `SHAP`
Leakage-safe Random Forest on 30,162 UCI Adult records (86.69% accuracy, AUC 0.924), audited by sex, race and both combined. ExponentiatedGradient cut the sex Demographic Parity gap from 0.154 to 0.004 for 2.2 points of accuracy, and I report what it did not fix. → [Adult-Income](https://github.com/xtames910/Adult-Income)

**🛍️ Customer Segmentation on Walmart Purchase Data** · `Python` `scikit-learn` `Pandas` `Seaborn`
Led a team of 3 to cluster 50,000 customers with K-Means (k = 4 by elbow and silhouette), then profiled each segment with a PCA view and a retention or remarketing action.
